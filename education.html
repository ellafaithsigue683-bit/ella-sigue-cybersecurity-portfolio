My formal education is in cinema, and cybersecurity is an area of continued professional development. My interest in video games has also made me curious about how digital systems generate, process, and store data, and how that data can be protected.

My interest in cybersecurity grew from two connected concerns: protecting my own digital life and understanding the systems behind the technologies I use. Video games sparked questions about how game systems generate, process, transmit, and store data, and what it takes to keep that data secure. That curiosity has developed into structured cybersecurity study, with a practical focus on protecting personal information and digital assets while building skills in security operations and threat monitoring.
