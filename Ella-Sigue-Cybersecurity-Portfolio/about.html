FILMMAKER & MULTIMEDIA ARTIST · DEVELOPING TOWARD CYBERSECURITY

My interest in cybersecurity grew in part from video games. I became curious about how games generate, exchange, and store data, how the systems behind them work, and how player accounts, sessions, and digital assets can be protected. That curiosity has developed into a broader interest in how digital systems handle information and how security practices reduce risk.

My filmmaking career gives that interest a practical foundation. Creative work depends on personal accounts, client communications, footage, project files, editing workstations, cloud storage, and archives. I am building skills in log review, network analysis, and evidence-based reporting to strengthen how I protect personal data and creative work while preparing for a potential path into security operations.
